Meetup signup guide

Fill the Google Form first (Name, SRN, Department), then follow these screenshots to sign up on Meetup and RSVP to AWS IGNITE.

Open Google Form Open Meetup AWS IGNITE event
  1. 1

    Open Meetup and sign up

    Go to meetup.com and tap Sign up or Join Meetup.

  2. 2

    Allow location

    If your browser asks for location, tap Allow so Meetup can show events near you.

  3. 3

    Enter your details

    Add your name, set location to Bangalore, IN, confirm you are 18+, then tap Sign up.

  4. 4

    Choose Attend events

    Select Attend events, then tap Continue.

  5. 5

    Use the free plan

    Skip Meetup+. Tap continue with free plan — no payment.

Required before the next steps

Open the AWS IGNITE event link

After signup, open this event page, then continue with steps 6–8 to tap Attend and confirm you are Going.

Open AWS IGNITE event
  1. 6

    Tap Attend

    On the AWS IGNITE event page, tap Attend.

  2. 7

    Tap Submit

    Confirm your RSVP and tap Submit.

  3. 8

    Confirm you are Going

    Make sure the event shows you as Going. You’re registered.
